VERIFICATION NOTES
- Total of 383 programs is consistent with the NCAA's ~389 sponsoring
  schools in 2022 minus subsequent closures (Birmingham-Southern, Cabrini,
  Wells, Medaille, Notre Dame College OH, Eastern Nazarene, Fontbonne,
  Northland, Clarks Summit, Bryn Athyn athletics) and D2/D1 departures
  (Ferrum, UT Dallas, Sul Ross State, College of Staten Island), plus
  recent additions (Hartford, Mary Baldwin, Regent, Asbury, Lyon,
  JWU-Charlotte, UW-River Falls reinstatement, St. Scholastica et al.).
- Schools whose first season comes in 2027 are excluded: Saint Francis
  (PA), Neumann-to-MAC move, Marywood-to-MAC move, Carlow, Luther-to-MWC
  move (Luther listed under its 2026 conference, the American Rivers).
- Members without baseball are excluded (e.g. Connecticut College,
  Goucher, Lake Forest, Carnegie Mellon, Buffalo State, SUNY Potsdam,
  Colorado College, UC Santa Cruz, Pratt Institute, Cedar Crest,
  Emmanuel, Regis MA, Maine Maritime — dropped baseball).
